The problem

You just earned your first money. Better games just unlocked. You want to play.

Then Freecash stops you seven times to ask for something.

A streak, two freezes, a prize case, a friend invite, a goal, a home-screen widget. Each one opens on top of the games you came back for.

The detail that hurts most

The payoff hides behind adjectives.

The goals read Good, Great, Incredible, Unstoppable. What a goal is worth, “25x more likely to earn $100” for 30 days, shows up only after you tap it.

So the sheet asks for a 30-day commitment and keeps the reason off screen. A reward I can't see can't make me choose.

Observed
Why it hurts players

Seven things I noticed, and the principle behind each.

1

Ask in context

Ask for a permission when it's needed, and say what's in it for me. Freecash asks for notifications before I know what it does.

2

Cost of interruption

Seven pop-ups in 44 seconds break the one thing I'm trying to do: get to the next game.

3

Information scent

“Incredible” tells me nothing. “25x more likely to earn $100” would have made me commit.

4

Habituation

Three Claim buttons in a row that look the same teach me to tap without reading.

5

Consistency

Top sheet, bottom sheet, toast, top again. Nothing stays in one place, so nothing feels connected.

6

Foot in the door

Small yeses lead to bigger ones. A 30-day goal before my second game is a big ask, and too early.

7

Goal gradient

People push harder as a goal gets close. I was at 10.39€ of a 25€ cashout, and the app covered the next step.

All seven say the same thing: give first, then ask.

The asks are worth keeping

Streaks bring people back, friend streaks bring new ones. Stacked like this, they're easy to wave away.

Trust sells in this category

The brief says it. A pre-ticked consent box and hidden rewards spend that trust early.

AssumptionI expect players to drop off at step 3. I had no Freecash data, so you won't find any numbers here.
My bet

If Freecash hands out its rewards and asks

one at a time,not seven in a row
inside the page,not on top of it
reward first,so you see what you get before you say yes

then more players start a second game, and more of them say yes to each ask.

ProposalBecause the app stops competing with its own games. It's a bet to test, not a result.
The solution

The Next reward card: one card that holds the queue.

1

One ask at a time

Dots show where you are in the queue, 1 of 4.

2

Reward before the ask

Every step shows its price, in the same tag the Quests tab uses.

3

Inside the page, never on top

The card opens up where it is. No pop-up, no sheet. The games stay on screen.

4

One tap to claim

Winnings land in your balance right away. Streak freezes come along as a chip.

5

Saying no is easy, and remembered

“Not now” moves the ask to Quests, reward and all.

The order, from easiest yes to biggest commitment:Free reward›Personal goal + friend›Home screen›Notifications

What would prove me wrong: the yes rate per ask (B) drops, because a card is easier to ignore than a pop-up, and next games started (A) don't go up enough to make up for it.
